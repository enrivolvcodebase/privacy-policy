# Puzzle Pals — Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** Enrivolv IT Solutions Pvt Ltd, Bangalore, India
**App:** Puzzle Pals™ (Android package `com.enrivolv.puzzlepals`)
**Effective date:** [16 11 2026]
**Contact:** info@enrivolv.com

> Host this page on the Enrivolv website (for example `https://www.enrivolv.com/puzzlepals/privacy-policy`) and paste that URL into Google Play Console → *App content → Privacy policy* and into `AppConfig.privacyPolicyUrl`.

## Summary

Puzzle Pals is a jigsaw puzzle game for children aged 3–10. **We do not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information or data, from children or from adults.** The app has no internet access, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

## 1. Information we collect

**None.** The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send any information off your device. We do not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or any other third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

To work, the app keeps the following **only on your device, inside the app's private storage**:

- **Photos a grown-up adds** to make custom puzzles (cropped and resized copies). The original photo in your gallery is not changed.
- **Game progress**: stars, best times, unfinished puzzles.
- **Settings**: sound, music, levels, timer and similar choices.

This information never leaves the device. It is **not** backed up to Google Drive or any cloud service; we have switched off Android backup for the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it. You can also delete it at any time from *Settings → Reset progress* (with the option "Also delete my photos") or by deleting individual photos in *My Photos*. Both are behind the grown-up check.

## 3. Camera and photos

- **Photos:** The app uses the Android system Photo Picker, so it can only see the single photo that a grown-up chooses. It does not ask for access to your photo library.
- **Camera:** The app asks for camera permission only when a grown-up taps **Camera** to take a photo for a puzzle. The photo is used only to make the puzzle and is stored only in the app's private storage.

Adding photos is protected by a grown-up check, so children cannot add photos on their own.

## 4. Children's privacy

Puzzle Pals is designed for children and takes part in Google Play's *Designed for Families* programme. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The parts of the app meant for adults (settings, adding photos, links to this policy and to support e-mail) are behind a grown-up check. Tapping the privacy-policy or support link opens your browser or e-mail app outside Puzzle Pals.

## 5. Advertising and in-app purchases

The current version has **no ads and no in-app purchases**. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before release, use only Google Play Families-certified ad providers, and never show ads during gameplay.

## 6. Data sharing and sale

We do not share or sell any data, because we do not collect any.

## 7. Security

Your photos and progress are stored in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read. Since nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to protect.

## 8. Your choices and rights

All data is under your control on your device. You can delete individual photos, reset all progress, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You are still welcome to contact us with questions.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version at this address and update the effective date. Significant changes will be described in the app's release notes.
